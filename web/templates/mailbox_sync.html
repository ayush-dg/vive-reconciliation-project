How it works
1
Sync to Blob checks the shared mailbox for messages received since the last sync and downloads PDF attachments only.
2
Each PDF is archived to Blob Storage, dated by the email it came from.
3
Sync to Webapp looks at what's in Blob Storage and queues anything new for extraction.
4
Anything that failed extraction gets retried automatically on the next Sync to Webapp click, up to 10 attempts — after that it stays a visible failed job instead of retrying forever.
